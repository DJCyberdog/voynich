# Transcription policy
Original MS408 image/folio/region -> visible strokes -> uncertainty-aware grapheme choice -> named and versioned EVA or other transcription -> segmentation/tokenization -> statistics -> semantic hypotheses. All joins retain stable folio, side, line, region, source version and source convention. Distinguish glyphs, allographs, ligatures, gaps, unreadables, uncertain spaces, editorial marks and line endings. Do not merge EVA, Currier, FSG, v101 or editions silently. Maintain raw immutable source references plus versioned conversion rules. Store no large scans/transcriptions until rights checked. Compare alternative transcriptions before treating statistical patterns as manuscript properties.

2026-11-27 owner authorization: the explicitly CC0-covered public transcription originals may be stored byte-exactly under `research/sources/originals/`, with file-specific provenance and digest manifest. Other source assets require their own rights determination. This does not authorize publishing private review data or changing scientific status.
