# C-1A/C-1B — frozen exploratory protocol (2026-11-12)

**Question.** Can an explicitly semantic, compositional artificial generator (C-1A clarity vs C-1B economy) produce Voynich-*like surface features* that distinguish it from a mechanically copying null, without assigning meaning to unknown EVA text?

**Scope.** New artificial baseline inspired by owner discussion. It is not Timm/Schinner's A-13 algorithm, not a decipherment, and not a new Voynich reading. A-13 is the closest programme family for deduplication, not a claim that this is a reproduction of the author's implementation. All operations use Python stdlib; no downloaded executable.

## Frozen design BEFORE inspecting target folio
- Version C1-2026-11-12-freeze-1, fixed seed 421307.
- Exactly 25 fictional meaning roots, including dak=water/liquid and en=animal. The ordered combination dak+en is unambiguous, while en+dak is ambiguous between animal-derived liquid (LI) and animal in water (SU).
- Each artificial scene has a prespecified intended meaning. Cues tir/vek support LI; sal/rin support SU, in the two-token window on either side. A context cue contributes one vote. Meaning is determined before emission; not selected retrospectively.
- **C-1A:** emit li/su if the intended sense leads by fewer than **2** votes. **C-1B:** emit li/su if it leads by fewer than **1** vote. C-1A should never emit fewer markers than C-1B on paired identical scenes. Encoder and reader share frozen rules.
- Generate 800 fixed-seed artificial scenes; first 640 constitute development only (no fitting); last 160 are held-out for the reported model check. Positive control: paired generator and decoder on intact artificial scenes. Negative control: randomly reassign context cues across rows, preserving suffixes and their counts.
- **Mechanical baseline:** same 25 seed strings and held-out per-line token counts; for each subsequent token choose one of the previous four and replace one character with probability 0.70, otherwise emit a root. This is a deliberately simple baseline, not a fitted reconstruction of Timm/Schinner.
- Freeze descriptive features: mean/median token length; local exact-match, Levenshtein <=1 and prefix-2 recurrences within the preceding four words *of the same line*; top-two final-bigram share; final-bigram Shannon entropy; frequency ending in dy/ey/ol/in. Those EVA endings are orthographic **proxies**, never semantic markers. No best-of-feature cherry-picking.
- For the *first manuscript probe*, preselect **f4r** from the Zandbergen/Landini ZL3b-n IVTFF v3b source (header date 2025-05-13). Exclude uncertain comma-containing segments, non-ASCII/allographically uncertain tokens, annotation codes, labels outside line form. Period is word boundary; <-> is a rendering intrusion treated as split (sensitivity limitation). Take its paragraph lines unchanged from the source. Do not use them to tune the artificial model.
- Local full-file input, if obtainable legitimately, must be hashed and checked against its supplied expected hash when specified. If the complete file cannot be accessed in the execution environment, an **explicitly labeled hand-extracted subset**, verified against direct source lines, may support only an exploratory single-folio diagnostic. Neither a claimed owner SHA nor a subset SHA authenticates the full official file.
- Compare model summaries and manuscript summaries **descriptively only**. The sample is too small and the models' alphabets and length distributions are unmatched. No statistical p-value or selection of language, cipher, hoax, hybrid, or historical origin. A necessary, not sufficient, condition for a later model-level test is to match held-out line, glyph, and word distributions across independent transcription witnesses under equal parameter budgets.

**Falsification conditions.** Synthetic model is operationally contradicted if C-1A marks less often than C-1B on paired heldout scenes, or the frozen reader cannot correctly resolve synthetic scenarios with intact information. A high recurrence mismatch against MS 408 can undermine the current *unfitted* generator, but cannot refute compositional languages generally. Strong counterhypotheses: natural unknown language, transformed language or cipher, positional non-semantic generator, Timm/Schinner local copying, hybrids.
