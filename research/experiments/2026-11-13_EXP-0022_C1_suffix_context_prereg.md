# EXP-0022 follow-up — preregistered residual suffix/context discrimination (2026-11-13)

## Exact question and deduplication
After C-1A/B did not reproduce broad MS408 surface morphology, test a **necessary observable but not sufficient semantic consequence**: do surrounding word-ending classes predict a target word's suffix class **above** its own stable three-character prefix, word length, and line position? The artificial C-1 disambiguation mechanism predicts contextual dependence, but cannot be identified with Voynich without a semantic key. This is NOT a new "decipherment" hypothesis. Prior EXP-0018 (unmerged PR #26) already demonstrates held-out preceding-class gains above line-position/length controls; its test *does not condition on the target's prefix/body proxy or on both sides jointly*. A-13 Timm/Schinner is a different mechanism.

## Frozen before calculating this target statistic
**Data:** user-held ZL3b-n v3b IVTFF 2.0 file, expected SHA-256 `bf5b6d4ac1e3a51b1847a9c388318d609020441ccd56984c901c32b09beccafc`. Source attributed to René Zandbergen/EVMT; exact upstream file identity and independent glyph validation UNKNOWN. No new official Yale live manifesto call; SRC-0007 owner-canonical only. The previous C1 parse library is frozen; f-number/line mapping, punctuation exclusion, and ASCII-only accepted words must be reported. Source may not be republished without rights check.

**Sample:** within-line target token length >=4 and with at least one preceding OR following accepted token. Outcome classes: word ends in dy/ey/ol/in versus OTHER, in that precedence, exactly five labels. Word "body proxy" = first 3 ASCII characters (NOT assumed morpheme), target length-bin <=4, 5-6, >=7; position first/middle/last in the accepted token line. Context = previous and next token's outcome class, or boundary sentinel if absent. The target suffix never directly enters predictor construction except through its observed label for training/scoring.

**Folios and heldout:** group all parsed lines with same IVTFF folio ID, assign to one of five deterministic folds by SHA-256 of the literal folio identifier concatenated with the fixed string `C1suffix-v1` modulo 5. Each of five folds is scored exactly once, trained exclusively on other four folds. Folio grouping controls line leakage; source witness is not independently held out (already seen in prior exploratory C1 session). No hyperparameter selection using heldout scores.

**Models:** target outcome y predicted with a hierarchical categorical (5-way) frequency model trained on training folds only.
- B0: global 5-category frequency with Jeffreys pseudocount 0.5.
- B1: body proxy (first3), length bin, and first/middle/last with backoff P(y|prefix3), then global. Fixed Dirichlet backoff masses: 20 at prefix3 and 20 at full target-feature combination.
- C2: B1 plus the exact ordered pair (previous ending class, next ending class); fixed shrinkage 30 pseudo-observations toward B1, per (prefix3,lengthbin,position,prevclass,nextclass).
- Primary statistic: mean log(P_C2(y)/P_B1(y)) natural nats per heldout token, weighted by all eligible tokens, not macro folio or top examples.

**Positive control:** synthetically generate tokens with a preserved 3-char body proxy/length and context, but target label deliberately depends on preceding-context class with a fixed 90% rule. Training and heldout are distinct artificial folio groups. C2 should outperform B1 on positive control, with no manuscript interpretation.

**Negative control / null:** after fitting B1/C2 to original training data and scoring original heldout, permute the (prev,next) context **pairs** among eligible test tokens within the same heldout folio, target line position-bin and length-bin (not prefix), keeping target labels, prefix3, and all baseline features fixed. 199 deterministic RNG replicates, seed 131126; primary shuffle statistic is C2 gain over unchanged B1. This null breaks association of local context with target labels conditional on folio, token-length and line-position strata, while preserving that stratum's context-pair multiset. It does not preserve prefix-specific context distributions or section effects within folios, and cannot by itself adjudicate languages. Report strata nonmobile fraction.

**Bootstrap:** resample observed folios with replacement 500 times using seed 131127; calculate 2.5/97.5% percentile bounds for token-weighted C2-B1 gain. Report all five actual heldout fold gains and sample counts. No invented uncertainty.

**Decision rule (exploratory, no confirmation of semantic morphology):** eligible support requires (i) >0.01 nats/token overall added context gain, (ii) >=4/5 folio-heldout folds positive, (iii) bootstrap 95% lower bound >0, and (iv) observed gain > 95th percentile of the 199 null permutations. Failure means no evidence for a *predictively useful* C2 over B1 under this frozen design, NOT that all contextual languages are impossible. Even full success is support only for orthographic dependence and is non-diagnostic among natural language, cipher, constrained non-semantic copying, or hybrid models.
