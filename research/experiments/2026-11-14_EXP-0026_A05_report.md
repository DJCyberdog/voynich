## Competing models and exact prespecified falsification
- **Narrow H1:** A `q`-prefixed certain word occurs at the beginning of held-out continuation lines less often than selecting an equal-length certain token uniformly from the same line.
- **Operational H0:** for each line, first-token identity is exchangeable among same-length tokens in that line. Conditional probability is `p_i = (# q-prefixed equal-length tokens)/(# equal-length tokens)`, and the number of q-first lines has an exact Poisson-binomial left tail under linewise independent exchangeability.
- **Decision locked before target:** `p_left <= 0.05` AND `(expected-observed)/N >= 0.015`. Stronger real-world countermodel: writing layout/paragraph-filling, section/hand-specific word structure or deliberate copy/abbreviation can bias edge tokens without a distinct phonetic script or historical cipher; H0 does **not** control all such mechanisms. If H1 passes, this refutes ONLY the specified exchangeable line-composition null in this editorial sample, not other nonsemantic generators.
