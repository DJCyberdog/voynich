This is a sensitivity finding, **VERIFIED** for the exact source parser, not a new confirmation of a manuscript-wide rule.

This heterogeneity is **DOCUMENTED from metadata / VERIFIED as source-level arithmetic**, but its cause is **UNKNOWN**. The strata are neither randomized nor independent of section/hand.

Both passed. Bootstrap percentiles are **descriptive sensitivity bounds, not confirmatory p-values**.
