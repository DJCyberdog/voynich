## Outcome and scientific boundary
**RESEARCH_COMPLETED, narrow prediction NOT MET; no independent decipherment or manuscript validation.** Frozen before scoring in [pre-run protocol](2026-11-22_EXP-0027_prereg.md), committed at `d6ddf6309f7bb879ddbacad44733a1e34d1e2acd`. It is *EVA IVTFF editorial text*, NOT verified MS408 stroke/glyph readings or independent transcription. Owner SRC-0007 Yale manifest remains the canonical working snapshot, not live authenticated.

**Single question:** does the combined predictor of `q`-initial × word-final y/n/l/other (8 states) use token **position** plus **previous observed class** to beat the better single-factor predictor, conditioning on editorial Currier A/B/unknown, word-length and line-length bins? This tests *predictive complementarity*, not directly generative language-vs-cipher-vs-copy mechanisms.

## Method
- Retain only `+P0` continuation lines with at least three entirely certain ASCII `[a-z]+` period-delimited tokens; reject uncertain comma, ambiguous markup and alternatives instead of silently choosing an interpretation. Exclude prior f3/f8 pilot roots. Input: 5,225 transcription rows total, 1,618 not `+P0`, 63 f3/f8 exclusions, 2,007 ambiguous/too-short lines after prior filters, **1,537 accepted lines / 12,167 tokens / 94 folio-number roots**. Roots are folio-name clusters, NOT independent verified physical leaves.
- Deterministic SHA-256 5-fold grouping by root; training excludes entire currently scored root groups. Fold-heldout root counts: 16, 16, 17, 19, 26. The source and many sections have already been exposed in earlier experiments; therefore this is **not a newly sealed heldout**, even though it is crossvalidated at root level.
- Four fixed smoothed `P(target class|features)` models: **B0** = Currier metadata + word-length-bin + line-length-bin; **P** = B0 + first/interior/last token position; **C** = B0 + predecessor target class; **H** = B0 + both. Predecessor in scored lines is the *true observed preceding class* (teacher forcing), so no claim of unconditional generation. Five-fold pooled negative log-likelihood in nats/token, lower is better. All shrinkage=25, global add-0.5 as preregistered. Fixed first 8 bytes SHA fold split; script runs Python stdlib only.
- Primary `D=min(NLL_P,NLL_C)-NLL_H`. Success required **D >= 0.005 nats/token**, improvement **D>0 in both Currier-A and -B strata**, and within-line/word-length-bin permutation **upper-tail p<=.05**. These criteria were registered before evaluation. Do not choose a different threshold after the result.
- Null 99 seeded permutations of the eight target classes **within each line and token-length bin**, preserving editor-group membership, token-length categories, line length and original within-bin line compositions. Not a substitute for section/scribe-aware alternative transcript checks. 2,000 root cluster bootstrap draws are descriptive only.

## Interpretation; hypotheses kept open
**VERIFIED within frozen one-source protocol:** predictive losses, strata and control computations; result matches defined script output. **CONTRADICTED only at model-scope:** the prespecified strong complementarity criterion `D>=0.005` **and** positive A/B is not satisfied. **INFERRED:** on this target, predecessor class is substantially more useful than token position under the specified baseline; the small hybrid benefit is predominantly Currier B. **UNKNOWN:** whether manuscript glyphs, actual hands/physical leaves, alternate transcriptions, or natural-language/cipher/copy-edit/hybrid generative origins explain the effect.

The **strongest counterhypothesis** is a line/template-conditioned copy-edit or position-aware cipher/orthography process: it could yield the same eight-state predictive profile without a distinct joint rule. The four predictors in this EXP are *statistical feature ablations*, not four independently implemented language, cipher, mechanical and hybrid generators. Their relative fit cannot choose among those mechanisms; a true head-to-head requires matched-complexity generators, independent source and physical leaf grouping.
