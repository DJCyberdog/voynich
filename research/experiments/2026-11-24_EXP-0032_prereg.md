B: character bigram with add-.5 global base, first/transition probabilities shrunk to global with weight 10; trained on full eligible training words. S: position-aware bigram, additionally condition transitions (including first) on coarse slot first/last/second/penultimate/middle (precedence first,last,second,penultimate,middle); shrink to B weight 20; stronger character baseline. Template model T: choose top 128 most frequent class sequences across TRAIN (sort count desc then tuple lexical), at most 128; within length use relative TRAIN counts over selected templates, emit independent units by class-specific TRAIN unigram within each class (+.5); mixture .5 template + .5 S when that length has selected templates, otherwise S. C: strong simple copy/edit competitor: mixture .5 S + .25 lexicon of up to 128 most frequent exact words across TRAIN (relative counts conditional on length; fallback S if absent) + .25 local exact-or-single-substitution copy from immediately previous eligible word if same length (half exact, half uniformly one position and a different unit among A-1; fallback S otherwise). No future neighbor or tuning. TC: mixture .5 T + .25 same lexical + .25 same local channel, identical mixture weights to C. T versus S tests templates; TC versus C tests their incremental value in presence of copy/edit.
