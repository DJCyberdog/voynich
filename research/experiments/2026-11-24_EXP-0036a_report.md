## Fixed methods
Longest-match restricted conversion using explicit native correspondences, rejecting entire token on uncertainty/unmapped syntax, retaining >=2 projected units; no rare @ codes or uncertain brackets. Distinguish alternate v101 minim codes rather than silently collapse. Source-specific mappings share a fixed union of 95 code types, not just intersection of observed types; 90 classes (five prior pairs mapped to codes), 128 BPE merges, max four units, same DP/position model and H alpha80. J=.5H+.5Q. No tuning. Same P0 and root hash. Token filter and alphabet differ from EVA tests; do not compare absolute NLL across representations. Markup barriers and strict rejection, singleton exclusion. A standalone ! piece is ignored as barrier marker and would anyway fail the >=2-unit requirement; token-piece counts are specific to parser, not exhaustive physical words. Exact locus IDs, not word alignment.
