# EXP-0001 — dating support versus inscription
Date 2026-11-05. Research question: Can parchment radiocarbon results uniquely date the inscription? Candidate HYP-0001. Alternative A: prompt writing on prepared support. Alternative B: delayed writing on previously produced parchment. Literature source Yale Beinecke institutional overview (SRC-0002); secondary discussion SRC-0003. No dataset of Voynich measurements acquired.

## Adversarial test
Premise: radiocarbon samples date biological material used as writing support. Suppose the support were prepared in the early fifteenth century and inscribed later. This does not change support age. Therefore same support date accommodates both alternative timelines. The proposed implication from material date to writing date fails regardless of statistical precision of parchment calibration.

## Outcome
CONTRADICTED **only** for sufficiency claim that support-date alone establishes writing-date. The actual time of writing is UNKNOWN; this does not invalidate historically contemporaneous inscription. Strongest alternative: independent palaeographic/art historical evidence may narrow the likely writing window, but this was not independently tested here.

## Method limits
A conceptual falsification of inference, not a new physical experiment or a manuscript decipherment. Positive control (independent dated ink application) unavailable. Null: unsupported writing date. Follow-up: obtain original lab report and explicit scope; inspect Yale manifest and pinned transcription data before quantitative claims.
