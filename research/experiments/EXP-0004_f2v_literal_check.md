# EXP-0004 — Folio 2v literal-word crosscheck (2026-11-05)

**Research question:** Does the independently published Currier C witness for f2v contain the exact sequences invoked as a literal EVA decoding example in the 2026-09-27 Celler Presse/Franke claim?

**Source (claim):** SRC-0008, https://celler-presse.de/2026/09/27/durchbruch-in-eschede-per-ki-kollaboration-das-raetsel-des-voynich-manuskripts-ist-entschluesselt/ , the section "Fallbeispiel 1 ... Folie 2v" explicitly lists standalone `pchor`, `qokedy qokedy`, `dal`, `qokedy qokedy qokedy`, `shey` with medical interpretations. This is **CLAIMED**, not a verified original glyph reading.

**Independent transcription presentation:** SRC-0009, https://vib.tamagothi.de/index.php?id=f2v&show=page . The accessible search representation displays eight Currier C rows labeled `f2v.P.1` through `f2v.P.8`, an existing Stolfi/interlinear-derived transcription. No manuscript text file was copied or added to this repository. Direct open yielded HTTP 500, so version checksums and independent raw-file parsing remain pending. This witness is not an original Yale glyph observation.

## Prespecified lexical falsification

If the published quoted strings are literal and relevant to that folio, a compatible witness should show the exact word `pchor`, plus adjacent runs of `qokedy` of lengths 2 and 3 at identified loci. Compare whole transliterated words; do not equate a substring or similar spellings to a literal token. Word segmentation, Currier-to-EVA normalization, and line-wrap marks can differ: note rather than silently repair these differences.

**Observed in web-indexed Currier C f2v.P.1–P.8:** P.1 begins `kooiin. cheopchor. otaiin. odain.`; the display has `cheopchor`, not a separate `pchor`; `qokedy` is absent in displayed rows, so its proposed repeated runs are also absent. Other visible words include `shol` and `sshey`; the latter cannot automatically be normalized to `shey`. These are textual observations of one transcription witness, not physical-glyph facts. No inference is made that `qokedy` never occurs elsewhere in the manuscript.

**Control:** `cheopchor` and `shol` demonstrate identifiable token boundaries in the witness (positive). Substring-only `pchor` within `cheopchor` and similar `qoteeey` are negative controls: they cannot satisfy an exact-token claim without a separately specified transformation.

## Outcome

**CONTRADICTED**, narrowly: the quoted f2v literal tokens/repetition examples do not agree with the available Currier C transcription witness. **UNKNOWN**: original glyph-level validity, alternative EVA transcription, whether Franke supplied illustrative rather than literal tokens, and all semantic/medical implications. This is a material discrepancy worth follow-up, **not** a comprehensive failure of the Eschede decoding model.
