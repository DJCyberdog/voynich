## Question and exact source provenance
Does the original 2004 paper by Gordon Rugg establish that the grille method *must* produce meaningless text, as the shorthand A-14 “Cardan-Gitter/Tabellengenerator ohne Inhalt” could suggest?

Direct original publisher abstract: Gordon Rugg, *An Elegant Hoax? A Possible Solution to the Voynich Manuscript*, Cryptologia 28(1), 31–46 (2004), DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/0161-110491892755 ; publisher page shows online posting June 4, 2010 (not original issue year). The publisher abstract expressly states the method can produce **either meaningless hoax text OR encode plaintext in a decodable cipher**. Therefore the *exclusive* interpretation “table-and-grille implies no meaningful content” is **CONTRADICTED as a claim about what the author proposes**, not as a manuscript-level falsification. Original article full text and specific table/grille parameters were NOT accessed. No rights assumed for reproducing its figures.

Independent author follow-up: Rugg and Gavin Taylor, *Hoaxing statistical features of the Voynich Manuscript*, Cryptologia 41(3), 247–268 (2017; published online 2016-09-13), DOI https://doi.org/10.1080/01611194.2016.1206753 . Publisher abstract claims Zipf-like word frequency, symmetric word lengths, and non-homogeneous word/syllable distribution are reproducible using a table-and-grille method. These are **CLAIMED** results, not independently replicated here. Author self-description: Gordon Rugg, 2013, https://hydeandrugg.wordpress.com/2013/04/14/visualizing-textual-structures-in-the-voynich-manuscript/ (prefix/root/suffix tables and moving perforated grille). This corroborates the *mechanism description* only.

## Falsification at the documentary level
- **Pre-specified source-level prediction:** If A-14's phrase “ohne Inhalt” is logically necessary for the proposed technique, Rugg's original abstract should not explicitly allow meaningful plaintext encoding by the same mechanism.
- **Strong counterhypothesis:** The grille is a *text production/encoding mechanism*, compatible with both meaningless output and an encoded message; meaningfulness must be established independently.
- **Positive control:** The publisher's original 2004 article abstract explicitly describes the table/grille technique as feasible for a meaningless hoax.
- **Negative control:** The *same original abstract* explicitly also allows a decodable plaintext cipher. This is a decisive counterexample to the stronger universal “must be meaningless” attribution.
- **Null:** Merely exhibiting a generator that produces Voynich-like word distributions cannot identify whether actual MS408 text is semantically meaningful, or whether that mechanism was used.
- **Held-out:** Manuscript folios, original glyphs, alternative IVTFF witnesses, synthetic output, original tables, and author code were NOT accessed or tested; no holdout prediction evaluated.
- **Result:** Source-level exclusive claim contradicted; weaker generative *possibility* remains CLAIMED, manuscript origin and semantics UNKNOWN. No language, cipher, hybrid, or nonlanguage family excluded.
