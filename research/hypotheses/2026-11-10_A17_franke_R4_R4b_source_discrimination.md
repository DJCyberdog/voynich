## Directly documented observations in author report

The author explicitly states **no demonstrated one-to-one correspondence** between individual text forms and 1, 2, 3, 4; units days/hours/angles remain unverified.

This is **not** equivalent to verifying the prior days/hours interpretation and is potentially testable without assigning any meaning to `qok`.
