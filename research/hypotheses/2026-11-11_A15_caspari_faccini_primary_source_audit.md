## Original publication, provenance, and locus
Gino Caspari and Agnese Faccini, *A Key to the Voynich Manuscript*, 2025 preprint hosted by Max Planck Society repository, primary PDF: https://pure.mpg.de/rest/items/item_3644902/component/file_3644903/content . The abstract claims a partial alphabet, **vernacular Italian in shorthand**, and a list of **400+ proposed words**. The authors explicitly say their key is **preliminary**, that rare glyphs and abbreviations remain ambiguous, and that a proper translation is future work (paper abstract, Table 1, Discussion and Conclusion). The authors explicitly **reject using EVA/v101 as the original glyph inventory**, because they believe some signs are incorrectly split/joined by these conventions. Hence an EVA-only reimplementation is a *separate transcription mapping hypothesis*, not a faithful original-glyph reproduction.

**Manuscript loci declared by authors:** star/constellation labels in Figure 1 (e.g. proposed POLAR, TOARE), folio **f66r** itemized list used for character segmentation, and Figure 2 sample lines **f2r.1, f88r.13, f24r.15, f102r.11–12, f106r.47, f2r.2**. Authors also cite f43r line 13 as an exception to the proposed `qo`/ `quo` contraction. These are AUTHOR-SELECTED discovery examples, **not blind heldout**. No source images, glyph crops, text transcription or lexicon copied into repo.

## Distinguish hypotheses and claims
- **CLAIMED:** glyph-to-Latin-letter mapping with some ligatures; context-dependent vowel/ending restoration; proposed Italian vocabulary and morphosyntax; proposed central-Italian origin. These are four different inference levels, not one verified decipherment.
- **DOCUMENTED:** original paper's statements, its examples, Table 1, limitations and methodological reliance on contextual visual and lexical choice.
- **UNKNOWN:** independent glyph segmentation on original Yale images, exact reproducible deterministic abbreviation policy, source-vs-EVA concordance, heldout performance, false-positive rate against rival language models, and unique semantic readings.
- **INFERRED:** an unconstrained shorthand/lexical-completion stage can create selection flexibility; the paper does not supply a blinded, prospectively scored comparison against equally flexible non-Italian alternatives in its visible methods. This does not by itself falsify Italian.
- **Workflow:** A-15 **WATCHING / SOURCE_REVIEWED**, not TESTED, not RETIRED.

## One discriminating prospective question and preregistration **for a future test only**
Can a **frozen author-faithful glyph+ligature+contraction parser** recover independent, context-blinded Italian lexical/morphological sequences on unseen MS408 folios **better than** matched Latin/Occitan/other Romance, synthetic cipher, and matched meaningless-text controls **at equal decoding degrees of freedom**?

**Primary falsifier:** after freezing exact glyph segmentation and permitted expansion rules on training folios, Italian heldout lexical+syntactic performance is not above matched alternative-language and structure-preserving nonsemantic nulls, with false-positive opportunities explicitly counted. **Strongest counterhypothesis:** flexible abbreviation restoration plus illustration-guided anchor selection and common Romance vocabulary can produce plausible-looking Italian fragments without a unique underlying Italian plaintext. **Positive control:** known contemporary abbreviated Italian manuscript text encoded with a frozen known glyph substitution. **Negative controls:** matched randomized lexicon, context-label permutations and a known non-Italian source. **Null model:** shuffle eligible lexical assignments while preserving word-length, sign-frequency, token-boundary and abbreviation-opportunity strata; estimate decoding score distribution without posthoc dictionary selection. **Heldout:** reserve folios never used in the author's examples or training; because the authors claim broad per-folio exploration, a truly author-unseen folio **cannot be guaranteed** and independent validation must be labelled appropriately. Freeze scoring thresholds before accessing labels or illustration content. **NOT EXECUTED** this cycle: no numerical claims, no fresh word frequency, no visual glyph verification, no translation.
