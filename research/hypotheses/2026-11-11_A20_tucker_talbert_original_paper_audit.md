## Narrow research question
Do the original authors give an **independent, reproducible diagnostic** that separates an early-16th-century Mexican origin from European natural-language, cipher, nonlanguage, and hybrid alternatives, without assuming the illustrated species or deciphered label in advance?

## Original author's claims, separated by data layer
- **ILLUSTRATION (CLAIMED):** authors identify 37 of 303 plants plus six animals and one mineral as New World; this is a *selected candidate count*, not 37 independently blinded identifications or a quantified specificity estimate (pp. 70, 76). At **Voynich f1v** vs **Codex Cruz-Badianus f9r**, authors propose similar caudex/root features but explicitly note differing leaf-base shapes; call one *Ipomoea arborescens* and the other *I. murucoides* (p. 71). **No independent image-blind taxonomic adjudication performed here.**
- **LABEL / GLYPH (CLAIMED):** **Voynich f100r plant 8** interpreted as *Opuntia*, label read `nashtli` ~ Nahuatl `nochtli`; **f100r plant 4** as *Yucca/Agave*, proposed reading `maguoey` ~ `maguey`; **f99r plant 28** read `tlacononi` (pp. 72–73). These are **author readings**, not a reproducible fixed EVA/physical-glyph mapping; an illustrative match chosen together with its word reading cannot be an independent heldout validation. The authors themselves call their syllabary preliminary and admit training as botanists rather than linguists (p. 73).
- **MINERAL / MATERIAL (CLAIMED):** **Voynich f102r** blue cube suggested as boleite; green pigment 'atacamite possible to probable' is attributed to the 2009 McCrone report, which is a **separate cited laboratory source not independently read in this cycle**. Mineral distribution and geographic provenance cannot be inferred merely from chemical identity (pp. 73–74). No source-complete chain of custody to Mexico is demonstrated here.
- **HISTORICAL ORIGIN (CLAIMED):** post-Conquest Nueva España (1521–ca.1576) proposed. The paper itself asks how parchment dated to animals killed in the first half of the 15th century could have been used over a century later (p. 76). This is an **explicit unresolved auxiliary assumption**, not a verified contradiction of reuse; pigment and parchment dates must not be conflated. Manuscript creation, parchment production, drawing style and later travel are different propositions.
- **TEXT:** no complete fixed alphabet, validated decipherment, independently verified Nahuatl identification or five-gate semantic proof presented in the passages inspected.

## Falsifiable future protocol (not executed)
**Prospective prediction:** After freezing the exact 37 claimed folio/plant assignments and all visual descriptors from the primary article, independent rights-cleared botanists blind to geographic theory will identify the nominated Voynich plant images as the *pre-specified New World taxa* more often than equally sized geographically mixed and artificial/composite medieval-herbal decoys under a locked score. Geographic distributions and dating must be assessed separately from text labels. **Strong counterhypothesis:** stylized/composite plants, selection from thousands of taxa, and post-hoc image-label matching generate apparent New World hits in any European or nonsemantic herbal. **Null:** taxon and provenance label exchangeability under image-matched decoys with identical annotator effort; control for leaf/root morphology, historical illustration style and known composites. **Positive control:** independently identified, rights-cleared known New World illustrations from the same artistic period. **Negative control:** independently known Old World plants plus generated composites, scored with exactly the same procedure. **Heldout:** reserve non-nominated Voynich herbal folios and unseen comparative herbals before locking annotation; do not treat author-selected f1v/f100r as heldout. **Falsifier:** New World-specific blind accuracy not exceeding matched decoy/null or failure of frozen taxon names on withheld folios. No numeric threshold retrofitted; threshold and annotation agreement must be preregistered before experiment.
