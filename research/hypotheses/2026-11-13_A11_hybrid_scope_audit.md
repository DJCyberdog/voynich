# A-11 — Scope audit: hybrid is a comparator, not an independently sourced historical claim
Date: 2026-11-13. Base main checked: `eee6721615098918cce02bf76e4fdda26db60176`. Existing PR #35 reused; no main mutation.

## Single bounded question
Does A-11 identify a historically sourced, independently falsifiable hybrid decipherment hypothesis, or only a broad comparison class? Priority: avoid registering an unfalsifiable umbrella as an independent discovery and prevent double counting A-03/A-08/A-12/A-13.

## Provenance and layers
Originating audit: A-11 in draft PR #33 at `c298262c8773d08e31ae8832141cafb05835f122`; its cited Bowern/Lindemann 2021 review DOI `10.1146/annurev-linguistics-011619-030613` is a *survey*, not an original author-specific hybrid cipher proposal. The originating audit explicitly calls A-11 an **integration alternative** not exclusively attributed to a historical person. This cycle did not inspect a new original manuscript image, verify Yale live manifest, ingest any transcription, or calculate a text statistic. SRC-0007 remains owner-canonical for indexing but not independently live authenticated. Manuscript strokes, glyph identities, EVA encodings, token boundaries and meanings all remain unmeasured in this review.

## Falsifiability audit and decision
Claim as registered: “two or more preconstrained mechanisms explain distinct folio/text classes.” With unspecified mechanisms, allocations, parameter budgets, and loss function, *every* observation can be accommodated by selecting components after seeing it. There is no unique observation that falsifies this umbrella. **INFERRED**: A-11 is not a separately testable manuscript claim *in this general form*. This is NOT a finding against any particular, frozen hybrid model, and not a proof of natural language, cipher, or meaningless text.

**Narrow counterexample to the claimed distinctiveness:** a single generator with section/hand conditioning can emulate apparent mixtures. Conversely a genuinely mixed synthetic corpus may favor two fixed generators. Both are compatible until models and evaluation budgets are frozen. A model-comparison result is about predictive adequacy, not historical provenance or semantics.

## Prospective test specification (not preregistered, not executed)
Before looking at heldout data, freeze exactly two explicit generators, their mixture gating features, common transcription version and parser, complexity penalties, source rights, and the fold assignment by *folio/section*, with training-only hyperparameter tuning. Prediction: penalized heldout log-loss of frozen two-component mixture improves over best single generator by a threshold fixed **before** measurement, with a confidence interval over folios. Null: one section-conditioned generator with comparable effective degrees of freedom. Positive control: independently generated synthetic two-regime corpus; negative control: synthetic single-generator corpus. Additional alternatives: natural linguistic morphology, homophonic substitution, self-copy generator and hybrid, all scored on same data with comparable fitting budgets. A failure rejects only the frozen mixture's incremental predictive claim, not the unrestricted hybrid umbrella. A successful mixture prediction does not identify plaintext, script, semantics, author or historical mechanism.

## Disposition
Evidence **INFERRED** (methodological non-identifiability); workflow **WATCHING**, audit stage **SCOPED_RETIRED** as *independent historical claim*, with A-11 retained as a **shared model-comparison control**. Do not create a new HYP or EXP ID, do not count a manuscript falsification, and do not count this as an independently verified historical hypothesis. Existing A-03/A-08/A-12/A-13 source findings remain distinct. Next substantive step: freeze one concrete author-proposed hybrid with primary-source parameters or reuse A-13/A-08 in a common heldout comparison; do not revisit the unfrozen umbrella.
