## Strong competing explanations, falsification conditions and controls for the NEXT experiment (NOT RUN)

- **A-13 Copy-T:** a source-fixed, genuinely causal word-copy/change model predicts manuscript-glyph/word/line recurrence and page drift after being trained on *only* training leaves.
- **Strongest counter:** a natural-language or cipher/hybrid model with scribal copying and multiple keys predicts similar statistics without implying meaningless text. A matched nonlanguage copy/edit null and manuscript line-layout effects are additional explanations.
- **Positive control:** generate labeled Copy-T texts from its *frozen, separately rights-reviewed code*, verify recovery of its known conditional copying signature on unseen synthetic leaf blocks. Do not run outside code before authorization.
- **Negative controls:** matched length/page/line-constrained shuffles, plus separately frozen natural-language and encoded-text simulators (single- and two-key), preserving editorial tokenization and computation budget.
- **Null:** after conditioning on layout, word lengths, Currier/section and per-leaf dependence, self-copy is *not* predictively better than matched alternatives on prospective manuscript leaves.
- **Holdout:** identify entire paired recto/verso physical leaves and foldouts via SRC-0007 concordance; stratify availability by Currier/section and reserve *before* W vocabulary/model training and threshold selection; prohibit all heldout wordtypes from training W and from choosing model hyperparameters. EXP-0026 prior holdout and previously exploratory inspected sides are **not** magically pristine. Obtain separate permitted image witness and two independent blind annotations; record uncertain glyphs and variant word breaks rather than collapsing to EVA.
- **Prediction/decision to preregister prospectively:** fixed model fit on training leaves alone must yield a measured improvement on unseen *leaf-level* features relative to both copy-only and multi-key cipher baselines, with frozen metrics, uncertainty and multiple-comparison treatment, and cluster-by-leaf inference. This manuscript-level criterion is **PROPOSED, NOT PREREGISTERED**; thresholds, seed, exact leaf IDs and holdout count await a rights/feasibility inventory and a future immutable protocol.
- **Falsification:** if frozen Copy-T falls outside predeclared holdout tolerances while a matched alternative remains within them, contradict that *specific* Copy-T specification, not all self-copying or the proposition of nonsemantic origin. If both perform similarly, A-13 is **UNKNOWN**. No source-level audit proves a language, decrypts a cipher, establishes semantics, or passes any of the five decipherment gates.
