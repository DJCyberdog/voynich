## Precisely bounded question and prediction

**Question:** Do Brewer's original *published research writings* already demonstrate or specifically identify a deciphered abortion-inducing recipe **in the Voynich manuscript MS 408**, as distinct from citing other historical medical manuscripts as plausibility analogues?

The narrow falsifiable **source attribution proposition** to check is: "Brewer/Lewis have shown at least one exact MS 408 folio/line and reproducible original Voynich reading whose deciphered text is an abortifacient recipe." **Falsifier at the source level:** direct author article scope/source tables distinguishing other manuscript cases and merely proposing a broader sex/conception motif, with **no** identified deciphered Voynich passage. ## Strongest counterhypothesis, controls, null and future falsification

**Strongest competing explanation:** The manuscript depicts or discusses anatomy, bathing, cosmology, plants or a mixture; the historical cultural setting and metaphorical imagery could resemble "women's secrets" without containing a single encoded abortifacient formula. Also possible: such a recipe *is* encoded, but its source and reading remain unknown. Neither is excluded by this review.

**Positive historical contextual control:** In Hartlieb's separate textual tradition the request to conceal contraception/abortion material is reported and historically located (Berlin Mgf 928 vs Munich Cgm 261). This is a **source attribution/genre control only**, not independent validation that a cipher was implemented.
**Negative historical control:** The *Breslau M 1302* 142r cipher, whose earlier abortifacient attribution Brewer directly challenges; test against original witness and competing transcriptions before treating its alleged content as VERIFIED.
**Null model for manuscript claim (prospective, not measured):** 'Women’s secrets' iconographic/contextual features and general medical secret-writing arise under historical mixtures with **no identifiable abortifacient Voynich plaintext**, so merely matching imagery or citing Hartlieb carries no discriminatory weight.
**Positive manuscript criterion (prospective):** Pre-register exact MS408 folio/line/region, a deterministic reading/decoder, word-boundary uncertainty, unambiguous independent abortifacient-specific predictions and historical controls; show repeated successful *blind* passage interpretation outside the chosen locus.
**Negative/heldout manuscript design:** rights-cleared, independent EVA/IVTFF witness comparison plus externally labelled nonabortifacient historical botanical/bathing/medical recipes, images from different folios and untouched folio groups selected **before** training. Balanced medical/nonmedical semantic controls and blinded evaluation; reject if outcomes are equally explained by uncalibrated narrative matching. Until a frozen decoding rule and locus exist, no actual discriminating MS408 test can be run, and **false absence does not refute the hypothesis**.
