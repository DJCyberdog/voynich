## Falsification and controls — prospective, NOT RUN

**Narrow pre-test prediction:** an independently annotated Rosettes diagram, registered by region before coding, will identify two **unambiguous solar motifs** in precisely the **far top-left** and **bottom-right** positions, with no similarly convincing alternative pair after the same coding criteria. The stronger *embryonic heat* interpretation additionally predicts that independently labelled period anatomical/embryological controls will exhibit the same relational composition more specifically than alternative cosmological/map diagrams. These predictions were formulated **after reading the original source**, so they are **exploratory design only**, not preregistered confirmatory evidence.

**Strongest counterhypothesis:** the two motifs are conventional astronomical, cosmological, decorative, geographical or mixed symbols; solar imagery need not imply an embryo. Another counterhypothesis is that the shapes are not independently recognizable as suns under blind coding.

**Positive control:** rights-cleared, dated, independently labelled late-medieval reproductive/anatomical illustrations with explicitly identified solar heat imagery, if obtainable; historical source attestation of Aristotle's relevant passage required separately. **Negative controls:** dated astronomical, cosmological and cartographic diagrams with two solar/star-like motifs but no embryology. Controls must be matched for scale, visual complexity and candidate symbol count.

**Null model:** in nonembryological diagrams, two sun-like forms may occupy arbitrary positions, including far top-left and bottom-right, with nonzero chance. Freeze positional bins, motif-class uncertainty, a matched comparison sample and threshold *before* seeing blinded labels. An image-only positive is not evidence for a particular deciphered plaintext.
